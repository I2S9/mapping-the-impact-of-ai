1. The project

Artificial Intelligence has moved in a few years from research labs to everyday tools used by companies, governments and individuals. This website explores, through a series of interactive data visualizations, where, how fast and for what AI is being adopted, and what this means for the economy and society.

We focus on three guiding questions:

  1. How are investment, research output and model development in AI distributed across countries?
  2. How widely do European enterprises use AI technologies, and how does adoption vary by country, sector and company size?
  3. Which tasks and occupations are people actually using AI for?

Data at hand

The visualizations are built on open, publicly available datasets:

  • Stanford HAI — AI Index Report 2026: global indicators on AI research, investment, notable models, policy and public opinion.
  • Our World in Data — Artificial Intelligence: long-run time series on training compute, model performance, investment and patents.
  • Eurostat — Use of AI technologies in enterprises (isoc_eb_ai): share of EU enterprises using AI, by country, size class and technology.
  • Anthropic Economic Index (Hugging Face): how AI usage maps onto occupational tasks (O*NET), by task and country.
  • OECD.AI Policy Observatory: national AI policies, skills, labour-market and investment indicators.
